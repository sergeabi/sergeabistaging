When insight alone is not enough

You may understand what feels misaligned and still struggle to translate that awareness into daily choices. A longer engagement creates room to observe what happens between conversations, adjust expectations and learn from your own experiments.

How the eight weeks work

The programme includes four private sessions, a personal direction map and structured weekly check-ins. Together we identify a manageable direction, review what you try in real life and adjust when circumstances change. The direction map is a working document, not a rigid life plan.

Who this is for

This format suits people who want continuity and are willing to take responsibility for experiments between sessions. The listed investment is $1,800. A free introductory call is a chance to discuss fit and practical arrangements, without an obligation to continue.

Try this for yourself

  1. Choose one change you want to explore over eight weeks.
  2. Describe what progress would look like in daily life.
  3. Identify time you can realistically set aside.

Questions people ask

Is this a course?

It is private guidance rather than a self-paced course.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Progress depends on individual circumstances, participation and the decisions you make.

Coaching is a reflective and practical process; it is not a substitute for medical, mental-health, financial or legal advice. No specific outcome is guaranteed.
