WHO AM I.

In 2018, I hit a wall. Burnout, misalignment, and a deep sense that I was living someone else's life. At an airport, I made a decision: STOP. I left the 9–5, sold what I didn't need, and set off to explore the world, myself, and what it truly means to live free.

Today, I guide people through their transformation — to reconnect, realign, and create a life that feels true.

MY STORY

I was burning my life. Running from one client to another, racing through airport terminals — until I stopped and asked myself where I was really going.

I left Paris and set off on a two-year journey around the world to discover who I was, inside and out. That journey is still unfolding through entrepreneurship, meditation, community, and guiding others.

Discover your direction. Explore new possibilities. Transform awareness into action.
