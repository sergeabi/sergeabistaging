02WHO AM I

MANY ROOTS. AN OPEN HORIZON.

I knew something
had to change.

In 2018, I was unhappy, burning out and caught in the rat race—running for the subway every morning, living in airport terminals and moving from one client to another.

I was burning my life.

On my way to another client, I was running through the airport. Then, all of a sudden, I stopped.

Which client? Which destination? What was happening to me?

I had a wake-up call!

I quit my consulting job, left my life in Paris and set off on a two-year journey around the world—to discover who I was, inside and out, and reconnect with my mission.

Today, I guide peoplethrough their transformation...

Are you ready to take the first step?

  1. Discover your power, true self and life mission.
  2. Explore endless possibilities to create your roadmap and plan your evolution.
  3. Transform your wounds and weaknesses into opportunities and wisdom.
