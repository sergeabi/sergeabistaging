Making space for a conversation in your own language

Living in France can involve decisions about identity, work, belonging and the future. Being able to explore these questions in English may make the conversation easier, especially when much of daily life happens in another language. This page is for people seeking personal coaching rather than relocation administration.

Online or Nice-based guidance

Serge Abi offers online life coaching and has a dedicated Nice coaching page. An introductory call can establish whether an online format or a locally arranged meeting is suitable. Availability and arrangements are discussed directly rather than assumed.

Coaching alongside practical support

Personal coaching can help clarify values, relationships, career direction and the choices you face. It does not replace immigration, tax, employment or medical advice. If your main concern is administrative, seek qualified practical support before making consequential decisions.

Try this for yourself

  1. Identify one challenge that is personal rather than administrative.
  2. Write the outcome you want from a conversation.
  3. Consider which format would be easiest to attend.

Questions people ask

Do I need to live in Nice?

No. Online coaching is available; local arrangements should be confirmed directly.

Can you advise on immigration?

Not through this coaching offer. Seek appropriate professional advice.

Coaching is a reflective and practical process; it is not a substitute for medical, mental-health, financial or legal advice. No specific outcome is guaranteed.
