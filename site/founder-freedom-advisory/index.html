The business is not your entire life

A growing company can quietly become the only place where a founder feels useful. Decisions about delegation, revenue or growth then become inseparable from identity. This advisory offer creates space to examine the business and the life it supports together.

Where we focus

We can explore competing priorities, the founder's role, boundaries around availability and the trade-offs of the next stage. Rather than assuming bigger is better, we consider what your definition of success requires and what your current operating habits make possible.

Engagement and fit

The homepage lists advisory from $3,600. Scope and arrangements should be clarified in an introductory conversation. This is not financial, legal or investment advice, nor a promise of business performance. It is reflective and practical support for founder decisions.

Try this for yourself

  1. List the decisions only you can make.
  2. Identify one recurring obligation someone else could own.
  3. Define one non-business priority you want to protect.
