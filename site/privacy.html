PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is Serge Abi, operating the website sergeabi.com from France.

For any privacy question or request, contact hello@sergeabi.com.

2. What data may be collected?

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • your first name and email address;
  • your Crossroads Audit answers, scores, result profile and selected life area;
  • your consent choices and the date and version of those choices;
  • information you provide when contacting us or booking a call;
  • limited technical and security information, such as IP address, device or browser information and server logs.

Please do not submit health information or other sensitive personal data through the Audit or contact forms.

3. Why we use your data

PurposeLegal basis
Calculate and deliver your requested Audit resultYour consent and the steps you request before receiving a service
Respond to messages and manage booked conversationsYour request and our legitimate interest in responding
Send optional follow-up and marketing emailsYour separate, optional consent
Protect the website, prevent abuse and diagnose errorsOur legitimate interest in operating a secure service
Meet legal, accounting or regulatory obligationsCompliance with applicable law

You may withdraw marketing consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in an email or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before it.

4. Who receives your data?

Access is limited to Serge Abi and authorised service providers that support the website and its communications. These may include:

  • Cloudflare, for hosting, delivery and website security;
  • Plunk, for transactional and consented marketing emails;
  • Aya CRM, for contact, consent and relationship management;
  • Calendly, when you choose to book a call;
  • professional advisers or public authorities when legally required.

External services such as Calendly, WhatsApp and linked social platforms process information under their own privacy policies when you choose to use them.

5. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where required, transfers are protected by an adequacy decision, approved contractual safeguards or another lawful transfer mechanism.

6. How long data is kept

  • Audit, enquiry and prospective-client records: up to three years after the last meaningful interaction, unless a shorter period is requested or a longer period is legally necessary;
  • marketing contact data: until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, with minimal suppression information retained when needed to honour that choice;
  • booking and client records: for the duration of the relationship and any applicable legal or accounting period;
  • security logs: only for the limited period reasonably necessary for security and troubleshooting.

7. Cookies and analytics

The website may use essential technologies needed for security and core operation. Non-essential analytics or marketing technologies will not be activated without an appropriate consent choice. If analytics are introduced, this policy and the website’s consent controls will be updated before activation.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise a right, email hello@sergeabi.com. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data-protection authority.

9. Security and automated results

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal data. The Crossroads Audit generates a reflective profile from the answers you select. It is a personal-development tool, not medical or psychological advice, and it does not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, providers or legal requirements change. The current version and update date will remain available on this page.
