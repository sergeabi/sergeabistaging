Life coaching rooted in Nice, open to the world

Nice has its own rhythm: the sea, the hills, a mix of local life and international journeys. Yet a beautiful setting doesn't automatically answer questions about direction, work or purpose. People here experience career changes, relationship decisions, business pressure and personal transitions like anywhere else.

I am based in Nice and offer life and freedom coaching conversations. If you're in the area, we can discuss whether an in-person meeting is practical; online guidance may be an option depending on the arrangement.

What can a life coach help you explore?

You may be balancing a professional transition with family responsibilities, trying to feel at home after a move, or wondering whether the life you've built still reflects your values. Coaching offers a structured space to think aloud and clarify the choices in front of you.

We might work on personal direction, habits, a decision you've postponed, boundaries or a more intentional relationship with work. We won't pretend the same formula works for everyone.

International perspective, local presence

My professional and personal journey has included consulting, entrepreneurship, long periods of travel and contemplative practice. That mix shapes the questions I ask about success, belonging and freedom. It doesn't make me an expert in every aspect of your situation; it means we can look at change from more than one angle.

Sessions are collaborative and practical. We can identify an experiment or next conversation that brings what we discuss into ordinary life.

How to start in Nice

The best first step is a conversation about your goals and whether my approach is a fit. We can confirm format, location, availability and any practical arrangements before booking paid work.

This page is for people genuinely seeking coaching in Nice. I don't claim additional offices or locations where I don't operate.

Try this for yourself

  1. Name the most important choice you are facing right now.
  2. Write what an acceptable outcome would look like, not only your ideal outcome.
  3. Bring that question to an introductory conversation if you want support.
