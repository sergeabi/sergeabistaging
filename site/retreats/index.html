The journey · Serge Abi

Space to reconnect with yourself and others

Retreats and conscious gatherings can offer something rare: time away from routine, shared attention and the possibility of meeting yourself without a crowded agenda. They do not need to promise miraculous transformation to be meaningful.

My interest lies in experiences shaped by presence, simplicity, community and thoughtful exploration. The format may vary from a small reflective gathering to a weekend of conversation, movement or contemplative practice.

What guides the experience

A meaningful gathering needs more than beautiful scenery. The quality of facilitation, clear expectations, appropriate boundaries and respect for individual choice all matter. Participation in any exercise should be voluntary, and people should know what is and isn't included.

Depending on the specific event, activities might include accessible meditation, shared reflection, time in nature or group conversation. We will describe each confirmed event separately rather than implying every gathering includes every practice.

Practical details matter

Before committing to a retreat, you should know the date, location, price, accessibility considerations, accommodation, safety information and cancellation terms. Those details belong on the individual event page or booking confirmation.

This is an overview of the experiences I am interested in creating and supporting. It is not a claim that an event is currently available for purchase. Check for confirmed announcements before making plans.

Begin with a conversation

If you're interested in future gatherings or collaborations, you can get in touch to explore what might be a fit. Private coaching can also offer a quieter starting point for people who prefer one-on-one reflection.

The invitation is simple: make space to notice, connect and bring what you learn back into daily life. A retreat is a beginning of practice, not an escape from living.

Try this for yourself

  1. Reflect on why you are drawn to a retreat: rest, learning, community or something else.
  2. Check the details and suitability of any event before booking.
  3. Think about one practice you could bring home rather than relying on the retreat setting alone.

Questions people ask

Are retreats currently open for booking?

Only events with explicitly published dates and booking details should be considered confirmed. Please inquire for current information.

Do I need meditation experience?

Requirements depend on the specific event; any prerequisites should be clearly stated before booking.
