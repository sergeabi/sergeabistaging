The journey · Serge Abi

Who I am, beyond a job title

My professional path has included consulting, entrepreneurship and the question of what makes a life feel free. For years I worked within structured environments and learned how to solve problems, navigate organizations and pursue ambitious goals. Those skills matter to me, but they weren't the whole story.

In 2018, after feeling depleted and increasingly out of alignment, I made a decision to change direction. I stepped away from the familiar professional path and began exploring the world and myself with more openness. The journey introduced uncertainty as well as possibility.

What travel and reflection taught me

Moving between places challenged some of my assumptions about status, belonging and success. I learned that freedom isn't only about movement, and that changing scenery doesn't automatically resolve the patterns you bring with you. Presence and responsibility matter wherever you go.

Meditation, reflection, relationships and community experiences deepened those questions. I don't claim to have completed some perfect transformation. I continue learning from the life in front of me.

Why I work with people in transition

I know something about the tension between outer achievement and inner misalignment. My coaching work offers a structured, human space for people who feel at a crossroads and want to make choices with more awareness.

My background gives me a perspective that combines practical planning with curiosity about the inner life. It does not make me a therapist or an authority on someone else's destiny. I ask questions, listen and help turn reflection into next steps.

An invitation, not a formula

If you're navigating change, you don't need to copy my journey. You may have children, a business, a home or a profession worth keeping. Your path may be quieter, more local or more gradual than mine.

What matters is discovering a way of living that feels true to your values and responsibilities. If that resonates, explore a private conversation or read my notes about change, freedom and conscious entrepreneurship.
