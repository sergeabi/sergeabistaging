When the decision is specific

A job offer, a move, a difficult conversation or a decision about a business can occupy more space than it deserves. This session is designed around one question rather than an attempt to redesign your entire life. Bring the decision as it stands, including the constraints that make it difficult.

What we can work through

We clarify what is actually being decided, distinguish facts from assumptions and map the consequences of the options. We can examine whether a reversible experiment would provide information before a larger commitment. The aim is to leave with a practical next step, not a decision made for you.

Format and investment

The Direction Intensive is a 90-minute private session priced at $300, as listed on the homepage. Start with a free introductory conversation to check whether the format fits. If your situation needs ongoing support or specialist expertise, a single session may not be the right choice.

Try this for yourself

  1. Write the decision in one sentence.
  2. List two options and the cost of delaying.
  3. Identify one missing fact you can verify.

Questions people ask

Do I need to prepare?

A short summary of the decision and any deadline is enough to begin.

Will you tell me what to choose?

No. We work on clarity and options; the decision is yours.

Coaching is a reflective and practical process; it is not a substitute for medical, mental-health, financial or legal advice. No specific outcome is guaranteed.
